## About me
My name is Arad, I am currently 20 years old. I'm interested in pure math (especially geometrically-flavored stuff like homotopy theory, algebraic topology, algebraic geometry, arithmetic geometry) and in the past used to do lots of computer science (including type theory, functional programming, robotics & low-level computer architecture).

When I was in high-school I tried taking calc 1 at Tel-Aviv University and failed miserably! Next semester I tried again and passed, and for the past ~5 years been (very slowly) working towards an undergraduate degree in pure mathematics. I spend a lot of my free time independently studying more advanced math topics.
