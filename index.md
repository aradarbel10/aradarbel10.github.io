## About me
Hello! My name is Arad, I am currently 20 years old and have roughly 2/3rds of a bachelors degree in pure mathematics from Tel Aviv University (as of summer 2025), though most of what I know is from independent studying on my free time. I'm very interested in pure math (especially geometrically-flavored stuff like algebraic topology, homotopy theory, algebraic geometry, arithmetic geometry) and in the past used to do lots of computer science (including type theory, functional programming, robotics, electronics & low-level computer architecture). I intend to use this webpage to blog about some things I am totally unqualified to blog about.
